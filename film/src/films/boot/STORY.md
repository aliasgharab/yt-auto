# BOOT. — story and shot list

**Logline:** A kid wants to write his own operating system from scratch. Every attempt crashes. His Shadow tells him he isn't smart enough. One blinking LED keeps him going, and on Day 41 his OS finally boots.

**Item after Day 1:** round glasses (he starts reading the manuals).
**Rep counts:** BUILD #n pops every time he compiles.
**Big hits:** TRIPLE FAULT and KERNEL PANIC (flash + speed lines), and the successful boot.
