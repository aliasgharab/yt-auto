It is a "what if" world story for teens and kids. Nobody else is voiced: other characters' words are
quoted inside the narration ("He pointed at me. Strike one.").

THE FORMAT (follow it closely; this is the whole formula)
1. Line 0 is the premise, spoken from the very first word, no greeting. Either a strange world with one
   rule ("In this world, ...") or something I did that had an absurd, huge consequence
   ("I ... and ..."). The viewer must understand the whole premise in one sentence.
2. The world's rules, fast: two to four rules, one of them a rule you must NEVER break. Short and blunt.
3. Concrete, funny, specific details that make the world feel real (how people eat, sleep, go to school,
   get married in this world; what my neighbor, my teacher, my mom do). Real names for a few people.
4. Rising stakes: real DANGER or a MYSTERY (something nobody talks about, people who never come back, a
   thing behind you, a rule with a dark reason) and/or a big prize. Escalate in steps. Hits on this
   format always have danger or a mystery; silly low-stakes premises flop.
5. A dark, clever TWIST ending that reframes everything before it and makes people rewatch (e.g. the
   prize is the trap; the narrator was part of the danger all along; the rule existed to protect
   everyone from the thing we thought was good). Plant clues early so the twist feels fair. End on the
   twist's final image in one short line. No moral, no sign-off, no question to the viewer.

STYLE
- $lo_w-$hi_w words TOTAL (about 2.5 minutes at fast narration), $lo_l-$hi_l lines. One sentence per
  line, usually 4-12 words, never over 16. Very short sentences. Punchy. Spoken. Lots of fragments.
- Kid-friendly: scary and tense is good; no gore, no swearing, nothing sexual, no real brands or
  real people, no copyrighted characters.
- Completely original: do NOT retell any existing story, fairy tale, movie or known YouTube video.
- Written for text-to-speech: no emoji in spoken text, no parentheses, no symbols; numbers as words.
- Every line has "speaker": "$N".
- Give a "popup" card to most lines (at least two thirds). The first line's popup is a "title" card.
  Cards SHOW the story with pictures: choose emojis that literally depict the line (people, places,
  objects, actions). Labels are 1-3 words.
- Title: the premise in 2-4 words, Title Case, plus ONE fitting emoji at the end
  (e.g. "Nobody Can Sleep 😴", "The Last Elevator 🛗"). Not a question, no clickbait words.
- Description: one sentence teaser of the premise, then #shorts #story #whatif. 8-12 tags.
